IT Helpdesk Agent

An automated L1/L2 helpdesk agent — it triages a ticket, grounds its answer in live external truth, and an independent critic gates the resolution before it auto-resolves or escalates.

Grounded in real, free, key-less APIs: endoflife.date (software EOL) · cve.circl.lu (CVE severity) · vendor statuspage.io (service up/down) — plus an internal runbook. It cites real EOL dates and real CVSS scores; it does not guess them.

The graph

ticket → classify → gather (lookup_eol · lookup_cve · service_status + runbook) → draft → critic ─(pass)→ finalize
                                                                         ^                    │
                                                                         └──────(revise)──────┘  (bounded)
                                                                                              │
                                                                             pass → RESOLVED (automated)
                                                                 revise-exhausted / policy → ESCALATED
Drafter gemma4:12b on Spark 1 · independent critic qwen3:30b on Spark 2 (one model resident per node, no load/unload). The critic is the automated gate — no human in the loop; it escalates what it can't clear or what policy reserves for a human (e.g. admin access).
